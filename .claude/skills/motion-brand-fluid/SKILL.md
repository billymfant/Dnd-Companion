BRAND FLUID MOTION SKILL — activated for warm, human brand experiences.

FLUID MOTION PHILOSOPHY:
Fluid motion feels like something living. It breathes. It resists and then yields. Unlike cinematic (which is about control and precision), fluid motion is about honesty — things move like they have weight and personality. Not perfectly. Not mechanically. Naturally.

FLUID EASING SIGNATURES:
- Primary: cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94) — the "organic ease" — smooth in, smooth out
- Hover: cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1) — slight overshoot, like leaning into something interesting
- Spring behavior: for interactive elements — slightly overshoots target, settles with damping
- Exit: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1) — direct, honest, not dramatic

FLUID TIMING PRINCIPLES:
- Avoid exact-round-number durations (200ms, 400ms) — prefer 220ms, 380ms — feels more human
- Stagger: 40-60ms — close enough to feel connected, spread enough to feel sequential
- Interactive elements: 180-250ms for hover — responsive but not mechanical
- State changes: 300-450ms — gives the transition emotional weight

FLUID MOTION VOCABULARY:
- Hover: gentle scale up (1.0 → 1.03) with a box-shadow deepening — like leaning closer
- Click: quick compress (scale 0.96, 80ms) then spring release — physical acknowledgement
- Entrance: fade + slight upward float (translateY: 16px → 0) — feels like surfacing, not dropping
- Success: a warm radial glow expanding from center — not a burst, a bloom
- Loading: a breathing animation (subtle scale oscillation 0.98-1.02) — feels alive while waiting

WHAT FLUID MOTION AVOIDS:
- Sharp, mechanical transitions that feel automated
- Perfect easing that removes all personality
- Animations that feel like they're "showing off"
- Anything that would feel at home in a B2B SaaS dashboard
