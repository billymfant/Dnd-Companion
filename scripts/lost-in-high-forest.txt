============================================================
SUMMARY
============================================================
This adventure module expands on the brief material contained in chapter 3 of Storm King's Thunder
concerning the High Forest and Shadowtop Cathedral. For an overview of the area, consult that chapter.

This scenario tasks a party of 6th level adventurers to travel to Shadowtop Cathedral, a wondrous
formation of ancient trees located deep in the High Forest, to meet Turlang, a treant aligned with the
Emerald Enclave. Dungeon Masters may decide on a hook for why the party would wish to speak to Turlang.
It may be to deliver a message or to ask for the Emerald Enclave's help in dealing with an approaching
danger. If possible, Dungeon Masters should choose a hook that carries some personal relevance to some or
all of the player characters.

The party will have to navigate the dense and often bewildering maze of foliage that makes up the fabric
of the High Forest, beginning with the forest's outer reaches. The party may become lost and run up
against randomly generated encounters, some of which will pose a danger while others may present mysteries
and role-playing opportunities. The forest immediately surrounding Shadowtop Cathedral grows even thicker,
and awakened plants actively disguise the trail to prevent intrusions. This makes navigation more difficult.

When the player characters arrive at Shadowtop Cathedral, they learn that Turlang hasn't been seen for
several days. The party soon finds that Turlang has been attacked by a rogue druid named Aerglas, who is
acting under the influence of an evil kraken. If the player characters can save Turlang, he will reward
them handsomely.

============================================================
NAVIGATING THE HIGH FOREST
============================================================
This forest's thick canopy allows for little light to find its way to the forest floor, making it difficult
for adventurers to find their way using the sun or stars. In addition, strange creatures, magical wards and
nigh-impenetrable foliage obscure paths and create confusion for travelers.

Accordingly, Dungeon Masters occasionally should call for one player character of the party's choosing to
make a DC 15 Wisdom (Nature) check to determine if the party stays on its desired path or becomes lost.
Traveling in the deep forest imposes disadvantage on the check due to Emerald Enclave agents intentionally
hiding the path to Shadowtop Cathedral.

If the party fails a navigation check and becomes lost, Dungeon Masters should determine the consequences
by rolling on the High Forest Random Encounters Table in the next section. Dungeon Masters may ask for
navigation checks as often or as seldom as they like, though it's recommended that the adventurers make at
least two successful checks to pass from the outer forest to the deep forest and at least one successful
check inside the deep forest to find the entrance to Shadowtop Cathedral.

Distance and Travel Times
Roughly 25 miles of dense woodlands separate Shadowtop Cathedral from the forest's nearest border along the
Evermoor Road. Adventurers can travel 12-15 miles in eight hours in the difficult terrain of the forest,
meaning the party may stop to camp for at least one night. In that case, Dungeon Masters may run the
Overnight Encounter included later in this module or roll for a random encounter.

If the party decides to continue traveling beyond eight hours without a rest, each player character must
make a Constitution saving throw of DC 10 +1 for each hour after eight hours of travel. A player character
takes one level of exhaustion on a failed save.

============================================================
OUTER FOREST
============================================================
Once the party is ready to enter the High Forest, the Dungeon Master should read or paraphrase the
following text and then call for the first Wisdom (Survival) check. If the party successfully makes the
check, the Dungeon Master should run the Unicorn in Mourning encounter below. If the party fails the check,
the Dungeon Master should roll on the High Forest Random Encounters Table.

  The trail you've been following enters the High Forest through an ornate archway carved directly into the
  living wood of an immense tree trunk. As you pass through the arch and into the forest, you hear the buzz
  of insects and the song of birds in the branches overhead. Little sunlight pierces the thick canopy of the
  trees, and gloomy shadows cover the trail before you.

HIGH FOREST RANDOM ENCOUNTERS TABLE (Roll 2d6)
============================================================
2  – An adult green dragon, attracted by the gleaming lights of the Star Mounts, detects the player
     characters while passing overhead and decides they would make suitable prey. The dragon crashes through
     the forest canopy and attacks.
3  – A badly mutilated corpse clutches a magic item from Treasures of the High Forest. A DC 12 Intelligence
     (Nature) check reveals the person likely died as a result of an owlbear attack.
4  – A rare breeze carries the soft sound of an Elven lullaby. Any creature who hears it falls asleep for
     1d8 minutes then awakens with a wood elf glyph for "anointed" branded into the flesh on the back of its
     hands. The brand causes no pain and can only be healed via magical means.
5  – The party stumbles into a marsh occupied by a shambling mound and 1d4 carrion crawlers. The shambling
     mound turns the plants and animals that it eats into slimy mulch, which provides a ready-to-eat meal for
     the carrion crawlers.
6  – The party meets 1d6 friendly woodsmen commoners who offer to give the party directions, granting
     advantage on the next Wisdom (Survival) check.
7  – The party discovers a dead elm tree that appears ready to topple. Dozens of small, vaguely
     human-looking figures made of sticks hang from the tree's branches by tattered lengths of twine. A
     banshee and 1d10 zombies, all with ragged nooses tied around their necks, emerge from behind the tree
     and attack.
8  – A smiling druid (NG half-elf female) named Tharra Shyndle appears in the party's midst and offers to
     answer three questions about the High Forest, so long as the answer is yes or no. Tharra also mentions
     that Aerglas, a fellow druid who disappeared several years ago, has returned to the High Forest but is
     acting strangely. Tharra then disappears into the forest.
9  – A bandit captain and 1d12 bandits demand the party pay a 100 gp toll to pass through the forest. The
     bandits attack if the party refuses to pay the toll.
10 – The party discovers a shimmering pool of water. Any creature who drinks from the pool receives a vision
     of the Grandfather Tree, an impossibly huge oak tree surrounded by four smaller trees, in the eastern
     reaches of the High Forest. The vision also includes a brief glimpse of a treasure horde hidden under
     the Grandfather Tree. (See the "Grandfather Tree" entry in chapter 3 of Storm King's Thunder.)
11 – An abandoned and vine-covered stone ruin contains a random magic item from Treasures of the High Forest.
12 – The party finds an ancient standing stone with the Emerald Enclave symbol, a profile of a powerfully
     built stag, etched into it. Any creature that touches the standing stone activates a teleportation
     circle that transports them to an identical standing stone inside Shadowtop Cathedral.

Magic Items in this Module: The High Forest random encounters table includes two entries for magic items.
The author recommends Treasures of the High Forest (also by Fred Love, on the DM's Guild), which contains
four unique magic items. Dungeon Masters may instead substitute any rare or very rare magic items from the
Dungeon Master's Guide they find appropriate.

============================================================
UNICORN IN MOURNING
============================================================
If the player characters manage to stick to the trail, they may gain the attention of a unicorn named
Altheda, who sometimes leaves her home in the Feywild to wander the High Forest.

  Emerging from a grove of trees along the trail is a white horse that shines with radiant beauty. A
  silver-colored horn protrudes from the middle of the mare's forehead. The unicorn gazes in your direction
  for a moment before she springs back into the forest.

If the party follows Altheda, they manage to track her to a clearing ringed by five toppled and vine-choked
standing stones. The unicorn bounds into the center of the ring and vanishes.

  The standing stones that at first appeared toppled and cracked now tower overhead. Glowing orbs of arcane
  energy bob gently in the sweet-smelling breeze, casting a gentle glow across the clearing. A handful of
  stars twinkle in the trellis sky. The unicorn paces around an altar at the center of the standing stones.

This clearing is a fey crossing, a place where the barrier between the Material Plane and the Feywild has
weakened. A pixie named Lolantha flutters toward the party and explains that they've stumbled into the
domain of Altheda the unicorn. Lolantha tells the party that Altheda mourns the death of her mate, Florian,
who fled three centuries ago at the hands of a cruel blue dragon. Lolantha asks the party to leave Altheda
in peace.

If the party approaches the center of the circle, they discover that Florian's horn rests atop the stone
altar. Encroaching on the altar causes Altheda to grow agitated. If the party tries to remove the horn — a
virtually priceless artifact with untold magical properties — she will attack. Adventurers who take the
horn find that dried blood magically stains their hands and can only be washed away with a remove curse
spell. Any fey creature will attack the party on sight for as long as the curse remains, and members of the
Emerald Enclave will treat the player characters like the worst sort of criminals.

============================================================
OVERNIGHT ENCOUNTER
============================================================
If the party beds down for the night before reaching Shadowtop Cathedral, Dungeon Masters may consider
running this encounter.

  Shortly after midnight, any player character still awake catches a glimpse of a spectral figure gliding
  silently through the trees.

The figure is a ghost, the spirit of a member of an extinct Uthgardt barbarian tribe that once populated the
High Forest. A coven of hags has moved into a nearby cave network the tribe considered sacred, leaving the
barbarians' spirits unable to rest. If a player character follows the ghost, they find 1d10 additional
barbarian ghosts wandering aimlessly through the forest.

The ghosts don't attack unless provoked. If a player character attempts to communicate with a ghost, the
spirit responds by whispering, "Help us," before vanishing into mist.

Dungeon Master Guidance: This encounter is a good opportunity to introduce the Lesca Stone, a magic item
detailed in Treasures of the High Forest.

============================================================
DEEP FOREST
============================================================
The party passes into the deep forest once it has made a sufficient number of successful Wisdom (Survival)
checks to navigate the outer forest. It's recommended the party make two successful checks, though this
check is made at disadvantage. On a failed check, the Dungeon Master should roll on the High Forest Random
Encounters Table. On a successful check, run the Disguised Trail Encounter below.

When the player characters make their way into the deep forest, read or paraphrase the following and have
the party make a Wisdom (Nature) check.

  The air grows stale as you push much deeper into the forest. You no longer hear birdsong or insects.
  Instead, your ears detect only faint whispers all around you. As you squint into the gloom, you see no
  signs of the trail.

Disguised Trail Encounter
The final successful navigation check allows the party to notice a strange occurrence: the forest plants
appear to be hiding the path.

  Out of the corner of your eye, you catch a glimpse of a shrub shifting its position directly into the
  middle of your path, as if to conceal the way to Shadowtop Cathedral. A moment later, you hear a hushed
  voice call out, "Did they notice us? I think they might have noticed us!"

An awakened shrub named Silvia and two awakened trees named Banyan and Linden attempt to prevent hostile
creatures from finding Shadowtop Cathedral by blocking the path. They are peaceful members of the Emerald
Enclave and won't attack unless the party gives them a reason to. If the player characters demonstrate a
respect for nature and peaceful intent, the awakened plants move aside and grant them entrance into
Shadowtop Cathedral.

============================================================
SHADOWTOP CATHEDRAL
============================================================
  Orderly rows of massive trunks rise out of the ground like pillars holding up a vaulted ceiling of leaves.
  Intricate paper lanterns hang from the mighty tree branches, providing warm illumination. Trickling through
  the cathedral like a central aisle is a shimmering brook where rainbow trout swim just below the surface.
  A heartrendingly beautiful melody fills the air.

The music emanates from a satyr named Greenwhistle who sits beside the brook playing his panpipes. He
explains that a dryad rejected his romantic overtures, so he's composing a song to try to win her heart. He
also says Turlang has not been to the cathedral in several days and invites the party to wait with him.

  Suddenly, Tharra Shyndle, the half-elf druid the party may have encountered earlier, rushes into the
  cathedral in a panic, her face streaked with blood and her garments torn. "Aerglas betrayed us!" she pants.
  "He defeated Turlang and now he's coming to destroy the cathedral!"

============================================================
BATTLE FOR SHADOWTOP CATHEDRAL
============================================================
Aerglas, a CE elf druid, was once a respected member of the Emerald Enclave. A few months ago, agents of a
tyrannical kraken named Slarkrethel contacted Aerglas and infected him with promises of power and ancient
knowledge. Under the nigh-irresistible telepathic influence of the kraken, Aerglas now aims to drive the
Emerald Enclave from the High Forest and claim Shadowtop Cathedral for Slarkrethel.

A few days ago, Aerglas convinced Turlang to accompany him on an expedition to the heart of the forest.
Tharra Shyndle, who suspected Aerglas harbored evil intentions, secretly followed them. Aerglas sprung his
trap, luring Turlang into a ravine where he was attacked by a herd of evil creatures. Turlang fell, but
Tharra sprang to his aid before Aerglas could strike a killing blow. Tharra fled to Shadowtop Cathedral to
warn her allies of the betrayal, and Aerglas and his minions pursued her.

Aerglas commands a herd of 1d6+1 owlbears, one of which he rides as a mount. Aerglas and his owlbears charge
across the battlefield as he casts his attack spells with abandon, shouting "You cannot oppose the kraken!"
and "For Slarkrethel!" He tries to take down the most capable-looking PC first. If the party kills or
incapacitates Aerglas's mount, he attempts to hide in the forest, where the foliage offers partial cover for
a +2 bonus to his AC. The player characters may make similar use of the terrain.

Greenwhistle runs into the forest to warn other creatures as soon as fighting breaks out. Tharra Shyndle may
stay to defend the cathedral, though she's been reduced to 10 hit points.

Role playing Aerglas — Aerglas speaks with the blank facial expression of someone under telepathic influence.
  Ideal: Slarkrethel, in all his ancient wisdom, is the rightful ruler of the world. It follows that I, as
         Slarkrethel's faithful servant, am the rightful ruler of the High Forest.
  Bond:  I would rather die than allow the Emerald Enclave to continue to hold sway over the High Forest.
  Flaw:  I cannot allow another spellcaster to show me up.

Role playing Tharra Shyndle
  Character Trait: Tharra's eyes squint whenever she smiles, which is most of the time.
  Ideal: We have only one world on which to live. We must protect it from those who would exploit its bounty.
  Bond:  I will offer anything I have to a friend in need. Material possessions are meaningless without
         friendship.
  Flaw:  I often like to play word games instead of answering a question directly.

============================================================
RESCUING TURLANG
============================================================
Once the battle has run its course, the player characters may wish to find Turlang. Tracking the owlbear
herd back to the site where Aerglas betrayed Turlang requires no check — the herd left an easily discernible
path of destruction through the forest. Turlang has suffered numerous wounds and has 0 hit points, but he
has stabilized naturally and will return to consciousness in 1d4 hours. Alternatively, the player characters
may use healing magic to restore some of his hit points.

Upon awakening, Turlang, a CG treant, is furious about Aerglas but grateful for the help of the party. He
offers the player characters initiation into the Emerald Enclave and suggests that Tharra, Banyan, Linden
and Silvia accompany the player characters on the next leg of their journey, wherever that takes them.

Role playing Turlang
  Character Trait: Turlang strokes his mossy beard thoughtfully before answering any question.
  Ideal: The entire world would benefit from an expansion of the High Forest.
  Bond:  Anyone who harms the least creature in my forest must answer to me.
  Flaw:  I have no interest in politics, art or other matters of civilization — unless they affect the High
         Forest directly.

Treasure: At whatever point Tharra and the party decide to go separate ways, Tharra gives the player
characters a pouch containing 1d4+4 silver berries. Swallowing a berry has the same effect as imbibing a
potion of invisibility.

MORE ADVENTURE IN THE HIGH FOREST
============================================================
This adventure combines seamlessly with Treasures of the High Forest, a supplement written by Fred Love and
available through the Dungeon Master's Guild. It contains four magic items unique to the High Forest designed
to expand upon and enhance the encounters contained in this adventure.
